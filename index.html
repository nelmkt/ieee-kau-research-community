Abstract—We're a student-led research community in the IEEE Student Branch at King Abdulaziz University. We help students take their first steps into research, pick up the skills they need, and meet the people who can help them along the way.

Index Terms—undergraduate research, research skills, mentorship, interdisciplinary collaboration, student community.

Who we are

The Research Community is a student-led group within the IEEE KAU Student Branch. We're here to make research a normal part of student life at King Abdulaziz University.

We give students a space to explore what interests them, learn how research actually works, and meet peers, faculty, and researchers. Through workshops, collaborations, and shared resources, we help turn early ideas into real contributions.

Our story

We started out curious about research, with no idea where to begin. Opportunities, resources, and guidance were hard to find, and research often felt overwhelming, or reserved for people who already had the experience or the right connections.

We realized we weren't the only ones. So we decided to build the community we wished we'd had: a place to learn about research, find opportunities, meet researchers, and grow together.

We believe research should be open to anyone willing to learn, whatever their background. Our goal is simple: make the first steps less intimidating and the rest of the way more doable.

Who is it for?

  • New to research: You're curious about research but don't know where to start or how to get involved.
  • Aspiring researchers: You want to build research skills, explore different fields, and find academic opportunities.
  • Already doing research: You're looking for mentorship, collaborators, and places to share your work.
  • Curious across fields: You want to meet people from other majors and explore ideas outside your own field.

Vision and mission

Our vision

To be a leading research community that connects aspiring researchers across disciplines and helps students everywhere explore and contribute to science.

Our mission

To build a supportive, collaborative space where students learn essential research skills, meet researchers, find opportunities, work across disciplines, and get the resources and guidance they need to grow.

Our values

  • Innovation & Creativity: We encourage curiosity, critical thinking, and fresh approaches to research problems.
  • Collaboration & Teamwork: We build partnerships and share what we know to reach common goals.
  • Continuous Learning: We keep learning, and we keep sharpening our research skills along the way.
  • Social Responsibility: We care about ethical research and work that gives back to society.

What we want to do

  • Promote research literacy: Teach the basics of research, its methods, and scientific thinking.
  • Develop research skills: Run workshops, talks, and hands-on sessions on academic writing, literature reviews, methodology, and science communication.
  • Make collaboration easier: Connect students with peers, faculty, and researchers for interdisciplinary projects.
  • Open doors to opportunities: Help students find research projects, mentors, and programs.
  • Support research development: Help students sharpen their ideas and proposals and work toward publishable research.
  • Build a shared resource hub: Collect research tools, databases, learning materials, and funding opportunities in one place.
  • Keep the community going: Encourage members to share what they know, mentor newer researchers, and keep the culture of collaboration alive.
About IEEE. IEEE (the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers) is a global professional organization that works to advance technology for the benefit of humanity. Its student branches and technical communities help students learn, work together, build skills, and connect with professionals and researchers around the world.

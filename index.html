Who we are

The Research Community is a student-led group within the IEEE KAU Student Branch. We're here to make research a normal part of student life at King Abdulaziz University.

We give students a space to explore what interests them, learn how research actually works, and meet peers, faculty, and researchers. Through workshops, collaborations, and shared resources, we help turn early ideas into real contributions.

About IEEE IEEE (the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers) is a global professional organization that works to advance technology for the benefit of humanity. Its student branches and technical communities help students learn, work together, build skills, and connect with professionals and researchers around the world.

Our story

  1. Where we started

    Curious, but stuck

    We started out curious about research, with no idea where to begin. Opportunities, resources, and guidance were hard to find, and research often felt overwhelming, or reserved for people who already had the experience or the right connections.

  2. What we did

    So we built it

    We realized we weren't the only ones. So we decided to build the community we wished we'd had: a place to learn about research, find opportunities, meet researchers, and grow together.

  3. What we believe

    Research for everyone

    We believe research should be open to anyone willing to learn, whatever their background. Our goal is simple: make the first steps less intimidating and the rest of the way more doable.

Programs

From your first workshop to your first paper, there's something for every stage.

Fellowships & Grants

Faculty Research Fellowships

Matches students with faculty and researchers who are looking for student collaborators on ongoing projects.

Development & Skills

Research Methods Bootcamp

Lab safety, hands-on wet-lab training, statistics and plotting, publishing as an undergrad, writing papers and abstracts, poster design, and presenting your work.

Development & Skills

Academia Talk, ON!

A series of talks, online and in person, with guest speakers from academia and industry on specialized and cross-disciplinary topics.

Hub

TheRepository

One place for databases, tools, scholarships, conferences, internships, and programs.

Hub

ResearchDigest

Our newsletter, every two weeks: upcoming events, national conferences, research opportunities, science news, member and faculty spotlights, and research tips.

Hub

OnlineHub

An online space where members share opportunities, resources, questions, and announcements across majors.

Community

PaperCafé

Think book club, but for science. Over food and drinks, members take turns presenting a paper they picked, then everyone talks it through. Every meetup has a different theme or format.

Community

Ambassadors

Motivated students who represent the community around campus, spread the word, and bring new people in.

Our vision

To be a leading research community that connects aspiring researchers across disciplines and helps students everywhere explore and contribute to science.

Our mission

To build a supportive, collaborative space where students learn essential research skills, meet researchers, find opportunities, work across disciplines, and get the resources and guidance they need to grow.

Our values

Innovation & Creativity

We encourage curiosity, critical thinking, and fresh approaches to research problems.

Collaboration & Teamwork

We build partnerships and share what we know to reach common goals.

Continuous Learning

We keep learning, and we keep sharpening our research skills along the way.

Social Responsibility

We care about ethical research and work that gives back to society.

What we want to do

  • Promote research literacyTeach the basics of research, its methods, and scientific thinking.
  • Develop research skillsRun workshops, talks, and hands-on sessions on academic writing, literature reviews, methodology, and science communication.
  • Make collaboration easierConnect students with peers, faculty, and researchers for interdisciplinary projects.
  • Open doors to opportunitiesHelp students find research projects, mentors, and programs.
  • Support research developmentHelp students sharpen their ideas and proposals and work toward publishable research.
  • Build a shared resource hubCollect research tools, databases, learning materials, and funding opportunities in one place.
  • Keep the community goingEncourage members to share what they know, mentor newer researchers, and keep the culture of collaboration alive.

How we're organized

Chair & Vice-Chair

Set the direction and strategy, lead the officers, and represent the community.

Secretary / Treasurer

Keeps the records, membership data, budget, and financial reports.

Outreach Chair

Brings in sponsors and partners, and builds relationships with faculty, researchers, and research centers.

Media Chair

Runs our visual identity, social media, and campaigns, and shares member achievements.

Webmaster

Builds the website, forms, and tools, including the fellowship matching platform.

Resources Chair

Finds internships, scholarships, grants, conferences, and research tools, and keeps the list accurate and up to date.

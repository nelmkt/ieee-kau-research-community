Who we are

The IEEE KAU Student Branch Research Community is a student-led community at King Abdulaziz University that promotes scientific research and a research-oriented culture among students.

We give students a place to explore research interests, build research skills, and connect with peers, faculty, and researchers. Through workshops, collaborations, and curated resources, we help students take their first steps into research and turn their ideas into meaningful contributions.

About IEEE

IEEE (the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers) is a global professional organization dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity. Through its student branches and technical communities, it helps students learn, collaborate, build skills, and connect with professionals and researchers worldwide.

Our story

We started as students who were curious about research but didn't know where to begin. Opportunities, resources, and guidance weren't always easy to find. Research often felt overwhelming, and at times out of reach for students without the experience or connections to get started.

We weren't alone in that. So we built a space where students can learn about research, discover opportunities, connect with researchers, and grow together.

Research should be open to anyone willing to learn, whatever their background or prior experience. Our goal is to make the first steps less intimidating and the journey after them more achievable.

Who is it for?

  • New to research

    Interested in research but unsure where to start or how to get involved.

  • Aspiring researchers

    Looking to develop research skills, explore scientific fields, and discover academic opportunities.

  • Research-active

    Seeking mentorship, collaboration, further development, and chances to share your work.

  • Interdisciplinary

    Eager to connect with people from other disciplines and explore ideas beyond your own field.

Our vision

To become a leading research community that connects aspiring researchers across disciplines and empowers students worldwide to explore and contribute to scientific research.

Our mission

To build a supportive, collaborative research environment: equipping students with essential research skills, connecting them with researchers and opportunities, fostering interdisciplinary collaboration, and providing the resources and guidance they need to grow as researchers.

Our values

  • Innovation & Creativity

    Encouraging curiosity, critical thinking, and original approaches to research challenges.

  • Collaboration & Teamwork

    Building partnerships and sharing knowledge to reach common research goals.

  • Continuous Learning

    Supporting lifelong learning and the ongoing growth of research skills.

  • Social Responsibility

    Promoting ethical research practices and research that benefits society.

What we want to do

  1. Promote research literacy
    Teach research fundamentals, methodologies, and scientific thinking.
  2. Develop research skills
    Workshops, lectures, and practical sessions on academic writing, literature reviews, methodology, and scientific communication.
  3. Facilitate collaboration
    Connect students with peers, faculty, and researchers for interdisciplinary projects and knowledge exchange.
  4. Bridge students to opportunities
    Help students find research projects, mentorships, and programs.
  5. Support research development
    Help students refine ideas and proposals and work toward publishable research.
  6. Build a shared resource hub
    Curate research tools, databases, learning materials, and funding opportunities.
  7. Sustain the community
    Encourage members to share knowledge, mentor new researchers, and keep a culture of collaboration alive.

Programs

From your first workshop to your first publication, there's a program for every step.

Fellowships & Grants

Faculty Research Fellowships
Connects students with faculty and researchers looking for student collaborators on ongoing projects.

Development & Skills

Research Methods Bootcamp
Lab safety, hands-on wet-lab techniques, statistics and plots, publishing as an undergrad, writing papers and abstracts, poster design, and presentation skills.
Academia Talk, ON!
Online and in-person talks, specialized and multidisciplinary, with guest speakers from academia and industry.

Hub

TheRepository
One central collection of databases, tools, scholarships, conferences, internships, and programs.
ResearchDigest
A bi-weekly newsletter with upcoming events, national conferences, research opportunities, science news, member and faculty features, and research tips.
OnlineHub
An online space where members share opportunities, resources, questions, and announcements across disciplines.

Community

PaperCafé
A relaxed paper club, like a book club for science. Over food and drinks, members present a paper they chose and the group discusses it. A new theme or format every time.
Ambassadors
Motivated students who represent the community, promote its goals across campus, and connect us with new audiences.

How we're organized

  • Chair & Vice-Chair

    Set the community's vision and strategy, lead the officers, and represent the community externally.

  • Secretary / Treasurer

    Keeps the community's records, membership data, budget, and financial reports.

  • Outreach Chair

    Builds sponsorships and external partnerships, and connects with faculty, researchers, and research centers.

  • Media Chair

    Shapes the community's visual identity, social media, and campaigns, and highlights member achievements.

  • Webmaster

    Builds the website, forms, and tools, including the fellowship matching platform.

  • Resources Chair

    Curates internships, scholarships, grants, conferences, and research tools, and keeps them verified and up to date.

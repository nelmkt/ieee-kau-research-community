Who we are

We're students from the IEEE KAU Student Branch. Most of us got curious about research, then spent months working out where to start: who to email, what to read, how a paper even gets written. This community is what we wish we'd found back then.

Our vision

To become a leading research community that connects aspiring researchers across disciplines and empowers students worldwide to explore and contribute to scientific research.

What we offer

What you get when you join, whether you're just starting or already deep into a project.

Workshops & training

Sessions on writing papers, doing literature reviews, picking a method, and the skills specific to your field.

Research with faculty

Faculty and researchers tell us when they need students on a project, and we pass it on to members.

Resources & opportunities

Tools, databases, scholarships, grants, and calls for participation, kept in one place so you don't have to hunt for them.

People to ask

Stuck on something? Someone here has probably been stuck on it too. Ask, share what you find, and meet people from other fields.

Goals & values

Innovation & Creativity

Ask the odd question. Try the idea that might not work.

Collaboration & Teamwork

Good research is rarely done alone, so we don't work that way either.

Continuous Learning

Nobody here knows everything, including the people running it.

Social Responsibility

Do research honestly, and make it useful to someone.

What we're working toward

  • Teach the basics of research, its methods, and how to think like a researcher
  • Run workshops, talks, and hands-on sessions that build real skills
  • Connect members with peers, faculty, and researchers across fields
  • Help members turn an idea into something they can publish
  • Open doors to research projects, mentorships, and programs
  • Keep one shared list of tools, databases, and funding
  • Have members who've done it help the ones just starting

Our departments

You can pick one when you register, or skip it and join as a member.

Media & Marketing

You'll work on

Visual identity, social media posts, campaigns, and event coverage.

You'll gain

Design and writing practice, and work you can put in your portfolio.

Tech & Web

You'll work on

The website, registration forms, and the ResearchMatch platform.

You'll gain

Experience building and maintaining things members use every week.

Public Relations

You'll work on

Reaching out to faculty, research centers, partners, and sponsors.

You'll gain

Practice writing to and meeting people you wouldn't usually get to talk to.

Programs & Events

You'll work on

Workshops, the Methods Bootcamp, PaperCafé sessions, and event logistics.

You'll gain

Experience running events from start to finish, and time with the speakers.

Resources

You'll work on

Finding and verifying opportunities, tools, and databases for TheRepository and ResearchDigest.

You'll gain

You'll get good at finding things, and you'll see new opportunities first.

Project Management

You'll work on

Timelines, tasks, and coordination across all departments.

You'll gain

Experience keeping several teams on the same schedule.
